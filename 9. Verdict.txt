9. Verdict
Rating: 4/10. I would reject this solution in its current form due to fundamental flaws in feasibility, security, and privacy that outweigh any innovation. It overlooks Indian agritech realities like existing verification ecosystems and rural tech constraints.
Top 5 improvements required before approval:

Integrate with AgriStack/Aadhaar for baseline verification, reducing reliance on custom geofencing.
Enhance spoofing detection with device attestation and metadata forensics, plus ban mock location enabling.
Improve rural accuracy via assisted GPS fallbacks and polygon geofences, with user-friendly error handling.
Add DPDPA-compliant privacy features: granular consent, data deletion policies, and minimization of location storage.
Simplify UX with offline modes, multilingual guides, and support for shared/leased land scenarios.
